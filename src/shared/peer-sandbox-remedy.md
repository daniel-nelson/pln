**When the reason is `<peer>-not-authenticated`, say what it means.** The peer is installed and answered its own probe with a refusal, which is a different thing from absent and wants a different fix: the credential store its CLI logs into is not readable by this process. The remedy is the user's, and `pln-config set peer_command` naming something this host can authenticate is the portable one. Say it once, where the substitution is announced, and continue with the substitute rather than stopping to ask.
